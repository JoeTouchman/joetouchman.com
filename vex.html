All projects Davis Senior High School · Robotics class · 2023–2025

VEX V5 robots.

Two competition robots built in class for the VEX V5 Robotics Competition. Year one with a teammate, year two on my own.

Year 1 · Game: Over Under · Built with one teammate

Catapult robot

The main mechanism is a slip-gear catapult: a gear with half its teeth shaved off. While the teeth mesh, the motor winds the arm back against rubber bands. When the gap comes around, the arm releases and hits the game piece across the field.

No other team in the tournament had a launcher, so we could score from our own side instead of driving back and forth.

  • Slip-gear catapult powered by rubber bands
  • Tank tread drive
  • Intake
  • 1st in qualifications
  • Won the 16-team school tournament
  • One match: 112–56
Year 2 · Game: High Stakes · Solo build

Pneumatic clamp robot

I designed and built this robot alone. It picks up a game piece with a roller intake, lifts it on a chain elevator and scores it, all with one button. It also has a pneumatic clamp.

I was the first in the class to get pneumatics working, and the clamp was the robot's biggest advantage in the tournament.

  • Pneumatic clamp
  • Chain elevator, one-button lift and score
  • Roller intake
  • Four-motor drive
  • Finalist in the school tournament
